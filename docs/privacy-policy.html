Privacy Policy

LifeWord · Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information LifeWord ("the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. LifeWord is built to work with as little personal data as possible.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account (by email and password, or by signing in with Google or Apple), we collect your email address and the display name you provide. This is handled through Firebase Authentication and is used solely to let you sign in and keep your progress tied to your account.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, payment card details, or any government ID.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your learning progress — completed lessons, streak, hearts, hints, and audio accent preference
  • Your profile photo, if you add one

This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. If you delete the app or your account, this data is removed.

What we don't do

  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

Subscriptions

If you purchase LifeWord Plus, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that part of the process.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app. This immediately removes your account from our authentication provider and wipes all locally stored progress from your device.

Children's privacy

LifeWord is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
