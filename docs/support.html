Frequently asked questions

How do I mark a word as "known" so it stops appearing in exercises?

Open the word's card (in a category or in the Dictionary) and tap the star / "Know" button. Known words stay visible everywhere, they're just skipped during practice exercises. You can un-mark a word the same way at any time.

I lost all my hearts — what now?

Hearts refill automatically over time. You can also switch to LifeWord Plus for unlimited hearts and uninterrupted practice.

How do I restore my LifeWord Plus subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same account you used to purchase, then go to Profile → Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your account and all progress. See our Privacy Policy for details.
